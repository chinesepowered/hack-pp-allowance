[01-problem] Cold open: the problem
NARRATOR: A teenager needs things. Notebooks. Sneakers. A calculator. Today a parent has two bad options: hand over a card with no limits, or be asked before every single purchase.

[02-intro] What Allowance is, and who it is for
NARRATOR: Allowance is a shopping agent for teenagers, and for the parents who pay for their school things. It pays with the family's PayPal, but only inside rules a parent signs. Meet Kip.
KIP: Hey, I'm Kip. What do you need?

[03-save-paypal] Dana saves her PayPal (Vault v3, JS SDK v6)
NARRATOR: Dana saves her PayPal once, in PayPal's own window. That is the Vault API: a setup token, then a payment token that stays on the server.

[04-rules-to-mandate] Plain-English rules become a signed mandate
NARRATOR: Then she writes the rules in plain English. The AI turns them into a structured mandate: a weekly budget, allowed and blocked categories, per item caps, and when to ask. A schema validates it, and Dana signs it. Change one rule later, and the signature breaks.

[05-notebooks] Request one: notebooks, paid at once
NARRATOR: Leo, who is fifteen, asks Kip for notebooks. Kip searches real stores through Channel3, with the price cap taken from the mandate. Code checks the rules, and Kip pays at once with the saved PayPal. No popup.
KIP: Done! It's inside the rules, so I paid.
NARRATOR: The receipt quotes the rule that allowed it.

[06-sneakers] Request two: sneakers over the cap wait for Dana
NARRATOR: Sneakers around fifty-five dollars are over the forty dollar clothes cap, so Kip does not pay. Dana gets a card with Kip's reasoning and the exact rule. One tap, and PayPal captures the payment.

[07-gaming] Request three: a gaming gift card is blocked
NARRATOR: A gaming gift card? Blocked, kindly, with the rule quoted. Nothing is searched, and nothing is charged.

[08-under-the-hood] Under the hood: PayPal calls and the agent trail
NARRATOR: Under the hood, every PayPal call is listed with its debug I D. The Agent Toolkit's create order tool is wrapped: the model can never reach it unless the mandate check passes in code.

[09-undo-and-pause] Undo (refund), the weekly note, and pause
NARRATOR: Dana can undo a purchase for twenty-four hours, which refunds it through PayPal. Kip writes her a weekly note from the ledger. And one switch pauses Kip at once.

[10-limit] One honest limitation
NARRATOR: One honest limit. In this prototype, Allowance charges the family's PayPal through its own sandbox merchant account, and the retailer order step is simulated.

[11-end] End card
NARRATOR: Allowance. An agent that pays, inside your rules.
